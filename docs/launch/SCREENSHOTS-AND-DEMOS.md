# Screenshots + 60–90 s Demos Prompt

**Ready-to-use prompt package for OrderRing, StoreCanvas, and OrderBay.**  
Copy any section into an image/video model, Cursor agent, or hand to a designer/videographer.

---

## Master Context (put this at the top of every generation prompt)

We are launching three self-hosted WooCommerce plugins:

1. **OrderRing** – SMS, voice & WhatsApp for WooCommerce (bring-your-own Twilio, zero markup).  
   Key flows: Ready for Pickup + Shipped status notifications, two-way order chat, STOP/HELP/START, quiet hours, bulk reminders, delivery analytics.

2. **StoreCanvas** – Self-hosted product personalizer / live canvas designer.  
   Key flows: multi-view canvas, option fields + conditional pricing, print-ready PNG/SVG/PDF, production queue, FPD importer.

3. **OrderBay** – Self-hosted ops desk: invoices, packing slips, delivery notes, shipping labels, tracking, item-level RMA, SLA, staff digests, e-invoice export (UBL/Factur-X – export only).

**Style guide**  
- Clean modern SaaS product marketing.  
- Light UI, high contrast, professional but friendly.  
- Prefer real (or extremely realistic) WooCommerce admin + frontend screens.  
- No stock photos of people.  
- Dark mode optional; light mode preferred for clarity.  
- Include brand names and short taglines where natural.  
- Do **not** invent features that do not exist.

## B. 60–90 Second Demo Video Prompts  
One focused demo per product. Target length 60–90 seconds, 16:9, 30 fps.

### OrderRing – ~75-second demo

**Opening text / narration**  
“OrderRing – SMS, voice & WhatsApp for WooCommerce. Bring your own Twilio. Zero markup.”

**Storyboard**
- 0–15 s: Order status changes to “Ready for Pickup”. Automatic voice call + SMS fire. Customer receives both.
- 15–30 s: Customer replies “Thanks!” — the reply appears instantly in the order chat history.
- 30–45 s: Staff opens Bulk Reminders and nudges remaining Ready-for-Pickup orders.
- 45–60 s: Dashboard analytics cards update live (sent / delivered / reply rate).
- 60–75 s: Quick cut to Settings showing quiet hours, A2P 10DLC guidance, and the line “You pay Twilio directly – zero markup.”
- End card: “OrderRing – for Twilio. Self-hosted. License never disables messaging.”

### StoreCanvas – ~70-second demo

**Storyboard**
- 0–12 s: Merchant opens a product and clicks “Start from a template” (T-shirt). Canvas appears with print areas.
- 12–30 s: Customer on the frontend customizes the shirt — uploads art, moves/scales it, chooses options. Live price updates.
- 30–45 s: Order is placed. Admin opens the production queue, sees the composite, and downloads true-DPI PNG + SVG.
- 45–60 s: Brief shot of the signed artwork proxy and the RGB / DPI disclaimer.
- 60–70 s: End card: “StoreCanvas – self-hosted product designer. One-time or yearly. 0% of your sales.”

### OrderBay – ~80-second demo

**Storyboard**
- 0–15 s: New order arrives. One-click “Print Invoice” + “Packing Slip”. Atomic number is assigned.
- 15–35 s: Staff adds a tracking number → customer receives email. Order is flagged “Needs Attention” when an RMA is requested.
- 35–55 s: Item-level RMA is created, RMA slip is printed, status moves to Approved → customer email fires.
- 55–70 s: Quick view of Documents settings highlighting the “Not tax advice” and “e-invoice is export-only” notices.
- 70–80 s: End card: “OrderBay – every order document + the fulfillment desk. Legal-minimum invoicing stays free.”

## C. Extra Instructions for the Generator / Designer

- **Aspect ratios**  
  – Screenshots: 16:9 or 3:2 at ≥ 1920×1080  
  – Videos: 16:9, 60–90 seconds, 30 fps

- Keep all UI text legible (zoom or crop carefully).

- Use realistic WooCommerce / WordPress admin chrome (left menu, top bar, etc.).

- Prefer actual screen recordings + light motion graphics over pure AI hallucination when possible.

- Do not invent features.

**Final deliverables expected**
- 8–12 polished PNG screenshots (clearly named, e.g. `orderring-dashboard.png`, `storecanvas-customizer.png`, `orderbay-invoice.png`)
- Three MP4 demo videos:
  - `orderring-demo.mp4` (60–90 s)

  - `orderbay-demo.mp4` (60–90 s)
