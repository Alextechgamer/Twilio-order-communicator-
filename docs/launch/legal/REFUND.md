# Refund policy

**30-day money-back** on Pro and Agency purchases.

Email the published support address within 30 days of purchase with the order/license key. We refund the plugin license fee. **Twilio usage, carrier fees, and A2P 10DLC registration are billed by Twilio to you and are not refundable by us.**

Refunds are not available for:

- License keys that have been publicly shared or used beyond the licensed site count after a warning
- Custom development or third-party apps

After a refund, premium updates for that key stop. Installed plugin copies remain GPLv2 software on your server; messaging (OrderRing) keeps working without a license.

This policy is the public 30-day guarantee in COMPETITIVE-GAMEPLAN §5.2.
