CardingDesk

When a card-testing attack hits your WooCommerce store, CardingDesk mutes the noise, purges the mess, and shows you the attack in real time. It is the incident-response layer your blocker doesn't have.

Buy Pro — $59/yr Agency (5 sites) — $129/yr

Problem

Card testers flood stores with hundreds or thousands of stolen card numbers. Blockers stop the next fraud order — nothing cleans up the 3,000 failed orders already stinking up your dashboard, the flooded inbox, or the report your processor is asking for. CardingDesk does not block checkout; it handles everything after an attack lands, and recommends a blocker to pair with.

What you get

  • Velocity detection — rolling failed-order window with a persistent wp-admin banner and optional auto Incident Mode
  • Incident Mode — one switch mutes failed-order emails (admin and customer); a daily digest replaces the inbox firehose
  • Attack visibility — failed-order velocity, top IPs, gateways, and countries; incident report export (Markdown + JSON) for your processor or host
  • Safe cleanup — dry-run preview, then purge to WooCommerce trash in batches with a 24-hour undo window
  • Status API — GET /wp-json/cardingdesk/v1/status for incident on/off, velocity, last attack

Complement, not replacement: pair with a checkout blocker (Checkout Shield, Turnstile, gateway fraud rules). HPOS supported.
